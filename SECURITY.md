Reporting a Vulnerability

We appreciate researchers, developers, and users who take the time to responsibly disclose security issues. Your report helps us keep EduLink safe for thousands of learners and educators.

📌 How to Report

To report a vulnerability, please contact the EduLink security team:

📧 Email : mthethwajimmyb67@gmail.com

📌 Subject: [SECURITY] Vulnerability Report

Please include:

A detailed explanation of the vulnerability

Steps to reproduce the issue

Impact assessment (what could happen if abused)

Proof-of-concept code, logs, or screenshots (if available)

Your preferred contact method

We encourage encrypted communication (PGP available on request).

⏳ Response Expectations

When you submit a vulnerability report:

48 hours — You will receive an acknowledgment

3–7 business days — Full analysis and follow-up updates

Critical vulnerabilities — Prioritized and handled faster

Fix timelines depend on severity, complexity, and dependency updates

We will keep you updated throughout the process.

🛠 What Happens After Review

If your report is valid:

The issue is logged internally

A patch or mitigation plan is created

A fix is developed and tested

A security release is published

You are informed of the resolution

You may be credited publicly (optional)

If the issue is not accepted, we will explain why — transparency guaranteed.

🔒 Responsible Disclosure

Please do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities until:

EduLink has released a patch

You receive written confirmation from the security team

A coordinated disclosure date is agreed upon

This ensures the protection of all EduLink users.

🎖 Optional: Recognition

If your vulnerability report leads to a fix, and you wish to be credited:

You can be listed in our Security Contributors Hall of Fame

Recognition can appear in release notes or documentation

Anonymous credit is also supported
